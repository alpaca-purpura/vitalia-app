## 2. Contract decisions

**D5 — Rail 4 buttons MVP-only:** `PanelLeftOpen` (toggle full), `Plus` (alert mock), `Search` (focus composer), `PanelLeftClose` (collapsed). F2 buttons (anclados/tareas/notas) hidden completely (no greyed affordance false). Adds F2 in dedicated story.

**D6 — Chat placeholder:** `ChatHeader` real (avatar Valeria + nombre + "En línea" dot) + 4 skeleton bubbles + skeleton composer + label "CHATSLOT · F1-S6". F1-S6 replaces body+composer, preserves header.

**D7 — Mobile hamburger:** NEW button TopBar `<md` viewport, handler `setValeriaState('full') + setShellMode('agentic')` (opens drawer). Drawer pattern: fixed inset-y-0 left-0, slide-in transform, backdrop `bg-black/40 backdrop-blur-sm`, close via Esc/backdrop/X/swipe.
